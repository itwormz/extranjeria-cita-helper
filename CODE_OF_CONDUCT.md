# Code of conduct

This project welcomes participation regardless of background, nationality,
identity or level of technical experience. English, Spanish and Russian are
welcome. People seeking an appointment may be under considerable stress; keep
communication respectful and practical.

## Expectations

- Discuss the problem and the proposed solution; give constructive feedback.
- Respect different experiences and technical skill levels.
- Protect personal information, including appointment and authentication data.
- Avoid insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks and unwanted contact.
- Do not post spam, appointment sales or offers to exploit other users.

These rules apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other
project-managed spaces. Criticism and disagreement are welcome when expressed
without personal attacks.

## Reporting and moderation

For a confidential report, open an issue titled **Private contact request** and ask
for a private channel. Do not include names, allegations, sensitive information or
evidence in that public issue. Share details only after a private channel has been
agreed. This route does not require publishing an email address.

As the maintainer, I may ask for changes, remove content, close discussions or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. I will consider the
context and seriousness of the incident and handle reports as privately as
practical. I cannot promise an immediate response.
